# Long Branch Farms launch setup

## Web3Forms contact form

1. Courtney visits https://web3forms.com and enters `longbranchfarmsky@gmail.com` to receive a free access key.
2. She forwards the access key to Casey.
3. In `contact.html`, replace `YOUR_WEB3FORMS_ACCESS_KEY` with that key.
4. Submit a test message from the live website and confirm it reaches `longbranchfarmsky@gmail.com`.
5. Mark the message as trusted if the first submission lands in spam.

The form collects the customer's name, email, phone, inquiry type, ZIP code, and message. The ZIP code helps Courtney assess delivery requests outside the normal 40 to 45 mile service area.

## Shopify before selecting a paid plan

- Add all products, prices, descriptions, and collections.
- Keep Bacon at zero inventory and mark it sold out.
- Create pickup and local delivery choices for the 5 lb Ground Beef Bundle and 10 lb Pork Bundle.
- Prepare local delivery for a 45 mile radius from 120 Long Branch Drive, Leitchfield, KY 42754.
- Add a message directing customers outside the delivery area to the website contact form.
- Configure farm pickup and explain that meeting times can be arranged directly.
- Add product photos later when labeled photos are available.
- Test the storefront, mobile layout, cart, notifications, taxes, and fulfillment settings before launch.

Some checkout, shipping, and delivery features may remain unavailable until Shopify asks the owner to select a plan. Stop before accepting a paid subscription or entering payment details.
