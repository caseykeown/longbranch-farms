Our Story

Raised with purpose.

A family farm built on honest care, local food, and the belief that you should know what is on your table.

Why We Started

Good food begins with daily care.

Long Branch Farms began in 2022 with a simple goal: give families in our community a dependable source for healthy, locally raised food.

We are two working parents raising a six-year-old while caring for our farm every day. We mix feed ourselves, watch over every animal, and make choices based on what we would feel good serving our own family.

That personal standard shapes everything we sell, from individual cuts to bundle boxes and bulk orders.

The Long Branch Difference

Natural practices. No shortcuts.

Pasture and woods

Our pigs have room to explore open pasture and wooded areas.

Feed mixed here

We mix our pigs' feed daily and supplement it with milk from our A2/A2 dairy cow.

Grass-finished beef

Our cattle graze on grass from start to finish for lean, flavorful beef.

No antibiotics

We raise our animals without antibiotics, vaccines, or added hormones.

Family raised

The same family that answers your message cares for the animals each day.

Community focused

We are proud to serve Grayson County and families across west central Kentucky.
